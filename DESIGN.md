## Overview
GoPick Manual is a documentation-first product UI that borrows the clarity of a modern SaaS dashboard without becoming visually busy. The visual identity is built on bright white surfaces, cool slate neutrals, and one assertive red accent that signals brand, primary actions, and active states.

The experience splits into two related moods. The landing page is slightly more promotional, using soft red and blue-tinted gradients, hover lift, and icon color accents to make the manual feel welcoming. The inner manual pages are calmer and more utilitarian: fixed headers, pale slate page backgrounds, white reading cards, and a strong emphasis on scannability.

This interface should feel orderly, contemporary, and dependable. It is not playful, luxurious, or highly expressive. The design earns trust by staying legible, spacious, and consistent.

## Colors
The palette is led by a single brand red and a broad slate neutral system.

- **Primary (`#e50914`)** is reserved for the most important action, active navigation, small emphasis labels, and branded highlights.
- **Primary Hover (`#b3060f`)** deepens the red for hover and pressed feedback without changing the visual language.
- **Neutrals** do most of the work: white and near-white surfaces carry the layout, while slate text establishes hierarchy from headline to metadata.
- **Dark Surfaces** appear only in focused areas such as the access section and the footer. These are deep slate rather than pure black.
- **Support Accents** such as indigo, emerald, cyan, amber, violet, teal, and blue appear inside feature icons, role badges, and status summaries. They are secondary accents, not alternate brand colors.

Use red sparingly. A screen should usually have one obvious red focal point, not many. The overall impression should remain neutral and documentation-oriented.

## Typography
The system uses **Inter** and a standard sans-serif fallback stack throughout. Typography is direct and practical rather than expressive.

- **Display and headline text** use heavy weights to create immediate hierarchy in heroes, section headers, and major page titles.
- **Body copy** stays in the 14px to 18px range with generous line height for long-form reading.
- **Labels and metadata** are often semi-bold or bold. The smallest labels use uppercase tracking for navigation aids, badges, and eyebrow text.
- **Monospace text** is used selectively for hierarchy diagrams and other structured reference content.

Type should feel crisp, contemporary, and easy to scan. Avoid decorative type treatments, condensed faces, or excessive weight variety.

## Layout
The layout follows a centered, fixed-max-width documentation model.

- Outer content sits inside a wide centered container with 16px mobile padding and 24px desktop padding.
- Major landing-page sections use generous vertical spacing, typically 64px on mobile and 96px on desktop.
- Inner manual pages use a five-column desktop composition with a one-column sticky sidebar and a four-column content area.
- Cards, grids, and repeated content blocks rely on 16px to 24px gaps, keeping the rhythm tight enough for documentation but never cramped.
- Headers are fixed at 64px tall, and sticky sidebar content begins below a generous top offset so it never collides with the header.

On mobile, navigation and sidebars convert to overlay drawers rather than collapsing the content into dense stacked controls.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is soft and functional. The UI does not use dramatic shadows or heavy skeuomorphism.

- Most hierarchy is created with white-on-slate tonal separation and thin borders.
- Standard cards use a very light shadow and pale border so they read as floating surfaces without feeling detached from the page.
- Hoverable cards add a stronger but still soft slate shadow plus a small upward translation.
- Overlays such as search panels and mobile drawers use deeper shadows and a blurred dark backdrop to establish focus.
- The dark access cards use translucent white surfaces over a deep slate gradient to add atmosphere without leaving the overall design language.

If a surface does not need emphasis, favor border-and-tone hierarchy over stronger shadow.

## Shapes
The shape language is soft, rectangular, and restrained.

- Most buttons, cards, drawers, and utility controls use 8px to 12px radii.
- Large content wells and special containers may stretch to 16px or 24px radii.
- Pills and role badges use full rounding.
- Corners should never become ultra-sharp or overly bubbly. The system wants gentle softness, not playful roundness.

This is a polished business interface, so shapes should support calm readability and familiar interaction.

## Components
### Buttons
Primary buttons are solid red with white text and a soft branded shadow. Secondary buttons are white with slate text and a subtle border, picking up red only on hover. Icon buttons follow the same white-card utility treatment.

### Cards
The system relies heavily on cards. Default cards are white with pale borders and light shadows. Informational sub-cards may switch to muted slate backgrounds. Hoverable cards lift slightly and increase shadow rather than changing color dramatically.

### Navigation
Top navigation is light and minimal. Default links are muted slate; hover and active states turn red. Mobile navigation appears in a right-side drawer over a dark blurred backdrop. Inner-page navigation uses a sticky white sidebar panel on desktop and a floating launcher plus slide-in panel on mobile.

### Search
The global search pattern is a compact white trigger in the header that opens a centered white panel. Results are rendered as muted slate cards with red hover emphasis, keeping the interaction aligned with the rest of the documentation system.

### Status and Role Markers
Role badges and icon panels use tinted color backgrounds with stronger matching foregrounds. These accents help categorization, but the surrounding card and page structure remains neutral so the screen never feels multicolored.

### Image Cards & Visual Placeholders
Workflow and operational documentation integrates standardized visual screenshot cards (`.manual-image-card`) to anchor instructions to visible user interfaces.

The component's renderer and styles live together in `shared/components/image-card/renderer.js` and `shared/components/image-card/style.css`. The component owns its shadow values and icon sizing; page-local data owns screenshot titles, captions, sources, and alternative text. See [Reusing screenshot cards](README.md#reusing-screenshot-cards) for asset loading and integration.

- **Container (`.manual-image-card`)**: Built on a clean white surface with subtle slate border (`border-slate-200`), rounded corners (`12px` / `rounded-xl`), and soft shadow (`shadow-xs`).
- **Placeholder State (`.manual-image-card__placeholder`)**: Used when a screen capture is awaiting creation. Features a dashed border (`border-2 border-dashed border-slate-300`), muted neutral background (`bg-slate-50/50`), center-aligned camera SVG icon, helper title, and instruction caption. Includes subtle hover elevation (`hover:border-slate-400 hover:bg-slate-50`).
- **Header Badges**:
  - Left badge: Identifies view name or feature context (`Screenshot: [View Name]`).
  - Right status badge: Amber warning pill (`Pending Screenshot` / `bg-amber-50 text-amber-700 border-amber-200`) when uncaptured; green pill (`bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 border-emerald-200`) when verified.
- **Caption Block (`.manual-image-card__caption`)**: Neutral text (`text-slate-600 text-sm`) providing context, highlights, or required fields visible in the screen.
- **Rendered Image (`.manual-image-card__img`)**: Full-width, lazy-loaded (`loading="lazy"`), rounded image surface with click-to-zoom readiness.

#### Image Gating Criteria
1. **Required Visual Anchors**:
   - Every top-level workflow module section overview (e.g. Candidates List, Activity Log Overview, Account Directory).
   - Core creation wizards, primary scheduling modals, and high-impact multi-step action flows.
2. **Optional Visual Anchors**:
   - Secondary detail tabs, single-field filter dropdowns, and text-only confirmation prompts.
3. **Fallback Policy**:
   - If an asset is missing or empty (`src: ""`), the interface must render the styled placeholder with the `Pending Screenshot` badge rather than breaking layout or rendering broken image icons.

### Page Structure & 5-Tier OOP Architecture
Inner workflow and documentation pages follow a strict Separation of Concerns (SoC) decomposed across five discrete layers:

1. **Layer 1: Data Contract (`dataFiller.js`)**:
   - Pure data dictionary attached to `window.__<module>Content`.
   - Never interacts with the DOM directly. Defines titles, descriptions, access paths, items, inputs, steps, rules, expected results, notes, links, and `images`.
2. **Layer 2: Data Store (`contentStore.js`)**:
   - OOP `class <Module>Store` managing section retrieval, deep search, and ID queries.
3. **Layer 3: DOM Renderer (`contentRenderer.js`)**:
   - OOP `class <Module>Renderer` constructing reading surfaces, badge indicators, access path breadcrumbs, callout boxes, and delegating image rendering to `GoPickImageRenderer`.
4. **Layer 4: Sidebar Tracker (`sidebarTracker.js`)**:
   - OOP `class <Module>SidebarTracker` generating hierarchical navigation and executing 140px offset scroll-spy tracking.
5. **Layer 5: Application Orchestrator (`behavior.js`)**:
   - OOP `class <Module>App` binding store, renderer, and tracker, handling URL hash routing (`#section-id`), and orchestrating any auxiliary widgets (e.g., RBAC tables).

## Docs Shell
All 15 inner manual pages use one documentation shell modeled on the shadcn/ui docs layout:
- a flat page tree on the left, with every `docsNav` group always open;
- the page in the middle: a title row with **Copy page** and previous/next arrow buttons, the description, the content, and a previous/next pager at the end;
- the page's own sections on the right ("On this page"): a plain two-level link list.

Inside the page, the content components follow the shadcn docs page (https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/forms/react-hook-form): bordered Steps, Callouts, tables, code blocks and screenshot frames, inline `code` for UI labels, and outline previous/next buttons. Each section heading carries a link-icon anchor that appears on hover and focus. `ui-dna.md` holds the page anatomy, the block mapping and the checklist for adding a page.

Shared owners:
- `shared/css/docs-shell.css`: tokens, base, header, layout, footer, motion.
- `shared/css/docs-navigation.css`: drawer, flat page tree, search dialog.
- `shared/css/docs-page.css`: page grid, title row and toolbar, heading anchors, "On this page", pager.
- `shared/css/docs-content.css`: section rhythm, headings, lists, links, groups and nested blocks.
- `shared/css/docs-components.css` and `shared/js/ui/docsComponents.js`: Steps, Callout, input table, inline code, access path, status, code block, icons.
- `shared/css/docs-flow-*.css` (overview, inputs, rules, journey) and `docs-screenshot-viewer.css`, with `shared/js/ui/docsScreenshotViewer.js`: the flow blocks shared by Accounts and Meters.
- `shared/css/docs-resolution-matrix.css` and `shared/js/ui/docsResolutionMatrix.js`: the meter resolution-paths matrix shared by Meters and Meters governance.
- Shell scripts in `shared/js/ui/`: `docsSidebar.js`, `docsPageTree.js`, `docsCopyPage.js`, `docsPageShell.js`, `docsTocTracker.js`, `docsHeadingAnchors.js`, `docsReveal.js`.
- Block pages: `docsContentStore.js`, `docsBlockRenderer.js` and `docsBlockPage.js` render any page whose `dataFiller.js` follows the Markdown-to-page mapping.
- The `docsNav` list in `shared/data/gopick-data.json` is the single source for the tree, the title arrows and the pager.
- `shared/js/headerSearchManifest.js` maps each page to its Markdown source. Search and Copy page both read it.

  - title `clamp(1.875rem, 1.6rem + 0.8vw, 2.25rem)`;
  - H2 24px, H3 20px, H4 18px;
  - lead 17px, body 15px (line height 1.7), small 14px;
  - inline code 13px.

### Rules
- Inner pages use shadcn 1px `--border` lines on callouts, tables, code, screenshot frames, chips and buttons. The landing keeps tone steps. Nowhere: gradients, glass, or shadows on anything at rest. Only the notes popover, which floats, has a shadow.
- No eyebrow labels or tiny uppercase tracked labels. Monospace only for inline UI-label code, code blocks, system output and the account hierarchy tree.
- How-to-use steps, journeys and wizard steps are vertical Steps in an `<ol>`, with red numbered circles or markers on a 1px line.
- Screenshot cards use the shared image-card `docs` variant: a bordered frame with title and caption bars, the image on a muted ground, and a fixed 2.1:1 media box, so lazy images never shift deep links.
- Classified inputs render as an Input | Type table. The data and the classification labels are unchanged.
- Search opens from the header trigger, any `[data-search-trigger]` element, or Cmd/Ctrl+K.
- Below 1024px the documentation menu opens from a round red floating button in the bottom-right corner (not in the header). It turns into ✕ while the drawer is open. It is the one floating control besides the notes popover, so it carries a soft shadow.
- Copy page copies the page's Markdown source (every source listed for it in the search manifest) to the clipboard and reports "Copied" or "Copy failed".

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do keep the UI predominantly white and slate, with red as the clear action signal.
- Do use spacing generously so documentation remains readable and calm.
- Do rely on borders, tonal separation, and subtle shadows before introducing stronger visual effects.
- Do keep component corners consistently soft and rectangular.
- Do render styled placeholders with `Pending Screenshot` badges whenever screenshots are not yet committed.
- Do maintain strict separation between data, store, renderer, sidebar tracker, and app behavior.
- Don't turn support accent colors into competing call-to-action colors.
- Don't fill a screen with multiple red elements of equal importance.
- Don't use heavy gradients outside hero or dark emphasis sections.
- Don't make hover states feel jumpy; motion should stay quick, smooth, and understated.
