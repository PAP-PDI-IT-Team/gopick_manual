# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

People Dynamics, Inc. is committed to providing a respectful, professional, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in this repository, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or other protected characteristic.

## Expected behavior

Repository participants must:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Give and accept constructive, evidence-based feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experiences, and technical backgrounds.
- Keep discussions focused on the repository and the work being reviewed.
- Protect confidential, personal, candidate, customer, and company information.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing credentials, candidate records, production data, proprietary material, or other confidential information.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, commits, and other project communication. It also applies when someone officially represents this project or People Dynamics, Inc. in a public or private forum related to this repository.

## Reporting

Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue, pull request, discussion, or social-media post.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the organization's approved private communication channel. Include the relevant context and supporting information without disclosing unrelated confidential or personal data.

Security vulnerabilities must follow the private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers are responsible for reviewing reported conduct and determining a response that is appropriate and proportionate to the circumstances. Actions may include:

- A private clarification or correction request.
- A formal warning.
- Removal of comments, commits, or other contributions.
- Temporary or permanent restriction from repository participation.
- Escalation through People Dynamics, Inc.'s internal procedures when applicable.

Maintainers must handle reports as confidentially as reasonably possible. Anyone directly involved in a report must not make the enforcement decision alone when another authorized maintainer is available.

## Good-faith reports

Reports made in good faith will be reviewed without retaliation. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves be treated as a violation of this code.
